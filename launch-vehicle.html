Team project · Individual contribution

Three decisions that moved the design forward.

These artifacts are selected evidence from a larger team project—not the complete milestones. The focus here is the challenge I received, the work I personally completed, and the result the team carried forward.

01

Engine selection

Validate a propulsion choice from an inherited baseline.

A teammate supplied the vehicle requirements and baseline data. I independently compared engine options and iterated the mass and geometry calculations until I identified a usable arrangement for Milestone 4.

Result · A propulsion choice supported by the vehicle’s mass and proportions.
02

Vehicle architecture

Turn separate constraints into one coherent vehicle.

I independently connected structure, materials, dimensions, component placement, and weight into an assembled and exploded hand layout. This was the most demanding phase of my work and became the configuration the team advanced in Milestone 5.

Result · One integrated architecture backed by calculations, not visual guesswork.
03

Visual communication

Make the approved configuration easier to understand.

The hand drawing documented the engineering decisions, but it was not the clearest presentation asset. I created a cleaner generative-image-assisted visualization from the approved configuration, and the team adopted it for the final poster.

Result · A clearer team-facing visual, explicitly presented as communication—not CAD.
