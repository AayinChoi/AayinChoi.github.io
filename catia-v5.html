Project brief

From physical measurements to a validated digital assembly.

Beginning with a physical Amazon stapler, I independently reconstructed its geometry, internal interfaces, and assembly relationships in CATIA V5. The final deliverable combines a complete 3D assembly with the technical documentation required to communicate every modeled component.

01 · Capture

Measure & decompose

Examined the product from multiple views, recorded critical dimensions, and separated the mechanism into reproducible components.

02 · Build

Model & validate

Modeled 10 parts, organized 2 subassemblies, and verified part-to-part interfaces within the completed assembly.

03 · Communicate

Document & deliver

Converted the finished geometry into 44 fully dimensioned drawing sheets covering the complete modeled system.
