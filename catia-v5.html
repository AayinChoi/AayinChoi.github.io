Project brief

Precision began before the first CAD feature.

I selected an Amazon stapler whose mechanism and individual components were substantially more intricate than those of conventional designs. Before opening CATIA V5, I used engineering measuring tools to create a hand-drafted dimensional record, captured values to two decimal places, and planned every part interface with final assembly in mind. That preparation became a single, reliable baseline for the entire build—no repeat measurements were required.

01 · Plan

Build the measurement system

Documented every required dimension by hand and established the modeling sequence, interface logic, and assembly plan before CAD work began.

02 · Execute

72 hours to first assembly

Completed 10 parts, 2 subassemblies, and the initial drawing set. At first assembly, only two interface dimensions required correction: 5 mm and 3 mm.

03 · Refine

Turn accuracy into evidence

With the core geometry validated, the remaining semester was devoted to refining 44 drawing sheets. The final project earned 198/200—the highest score across all sections taught by the instructor.
